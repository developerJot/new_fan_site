Portable lamp

Inspire

Light moves with you

BELO is made to be picked up. The straight, rounded body gives a steady grip, so you can carry it to the table, the shelf or the balcony and set it down wherever the evening is.

Every shade is blown and finished by hand.

A smooth powder-coated body that is easy to wipe clean.

Detail design

Glass in layers

The shade is blown from molten glass and shaped in a heated mould. Slow turning forms the ripples, so the light comes through soft and a little uneven, like water.

Detail design

Touch dimmer

A touch dimmer sits in the base. Slide your finger to go from a dim night glow to full brightness, with no steps in between.

Detail design

Made for daily use

With an IP44 splash-resistant rating, BELO is fine on a balcony or in a bathroom. The USB-C battery lasts through the evening.

A quiet companion through the day and into the night.

Built for living indoors and out, with IP44 protection.
